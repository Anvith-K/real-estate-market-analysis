# Real Estate Market Analytics

##  Overview

This project analyzes real estate market data to understand **property pricing, rental trends, location-based variations, property types, and market demand**.

The analysis focuses on identifying pricing patterns, comparing property markets across locations, and understanding factors that influence property prices and rental yields.

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** was developed to visualize real estate market performance and provide data-driven insights for property analysis and decision-making.

##  Objectives

- Analyze property prices across different locations
- Identify areas with higher and lower property prices
- Compare different property types
- Analyze rental prices and rental yields
- Identify pricing and market trends
- Understand the relationship between property characteristics and prices
- Analyze market demand across locations
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate actionable real estate market insights

##  Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard and data visualization
- **SQL** – Data querying and analysis

##  Analysis Performed

### Property Price Analysis

Analyzed property prices to understand overall market pricing and identify differences between properties and locations.

### Location Analysis

Compared property prices, rental prices, and demand across different geographical locations to identify high-value and affordable markets.

### Property Type Analysis

Analyzed different property categories to understand their pricing, availability, and market demand.

### Rental Market Analysis

Analyzed rental prices and rental yields to understand potential rental market opportunities.

### Price & Size Analysis

Examined the relationship between property size and property prices to identify pricing patterns.

### Market Demand Analysis

Compared demand across different locations and property types to identify areas and property categories with stronger market interest.
